An offline learning studio.

This is the shell and interaction proposal for Machine Room. It demonstrates a course collection, chapter learning, diagram exercises, guided labs, project gates, the C lexicon, and an open lab.

The sample course progress and check results are design scenarios. A compiler runner and the complete course database are not connected in this artifact. Open lab drafts are saved locally in this browser.

The planned app uses Rust and Tauri, with versioned original course content and progress stored on your computer. References, font assets, and diagrams are bundled locally.
